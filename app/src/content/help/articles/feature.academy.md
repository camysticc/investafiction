Each lesson has a short intro, a 'try it' activity, often a sandbox task using the real tools on a training case, a three question check and a reward. Wrong answers give a hint and another go.

XP: 20 per lesson, 10 bonus for passing the check first time, 10 for a sandbox task, 100 per track and 150 for the practice case. Ranks run from Trainee to Lead Analyst. Rewards are cosmetic and never affect case scores.
