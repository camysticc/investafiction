1. Open the evidence list.
2. Press **Log all** to log receipt of every new item.
3. Press **Hash all** to compare every item with its acquisition hash.
4. If any item shows **Hash ✗**, report it from the Integrity tab.

Do this at the start of every stage, because each stage releases new items.
