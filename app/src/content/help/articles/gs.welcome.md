Each case arrives as a **case pack**. You read the brief, log and check the evidence, use tools such as the timeline, link chart and map, record findings with citations, and write a report. The program then marks your work and shows a debrief.

Everything is fictional and everything stays on your computer. The program never connects to the internet.
