1. **Read the brief** and the intake form. Note what is in and out of scope.
2. **Log receipt** of every item (Log all) and **check hashes** (Hash all).
3. **Examine** items in the viewer. Grade the sources.
4. **Record findings** as fact, inference or assumption, each with citations.
5. Build the **timeline** and **link chart**, compare **statements**, make focused **requests**.
6. Write the **report**, sign the legality statement and **submit**.

New? Try the Academy's quick intro or the First Day practice case first.
