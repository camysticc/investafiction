Viewers exist for tables and logs, emails (with headers), phone extractions (messages, calls, locations), images (with metadata), documents and data responses. The header shows source, acquisition details, any clock offset and handling notes.

Tabs inside the viewer show **Content**, **Metadata** and **Chain of custody**. Grade the source and information with the controls at the top right.
