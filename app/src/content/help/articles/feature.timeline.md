Add events from table rows, phone records or statements, or by hand with **+ Event**. Filter by person or thing, export as CSV, a table or an image.

Uncorrected device times are a common trap. Check the item's clock note before adding a time. Sort order, grouping, gap markers and confidence colours are in **Settings > Timeline**.
