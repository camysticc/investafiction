1. Pick the statements to compare.
2. Select a claim and choose a relation: **agrees with**, **conflicts with**, **adds detail to**, **cannot be checked**, **supported by evidence** or **contradicted by evidence**.
3. Click the other claim, or choose the evidence.

The **Summary table** shows every claim as confirmed, contradicted, conflicting, consistent or unverified. A claim nobody can check is unverified, never false.

Credit goes for finding the real inconsistencies and tying them to the right evidence. Calling someone a liar because their account differs, with no evidence that they knew the truth, is the trap **Mistaking a difference for a lie**.
