Source: A always reliable, B mostly reliable, C sometimes reliable, D unreliable, E untested. Information: 1 known true without reservation, 2 known personally to the source, 3 not known personally but corroborated, 4 cannot be judged, 5 suspected false.

*This is a simplified explanation for learning, not official guidance.*
