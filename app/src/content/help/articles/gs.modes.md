Choose the mode when you start an attempt, in the library or with **Settings > Gameplay and mode > Mode**.

- **Training:** up to three hint levels per objective (each costs a few marks), and one warning before a trap.
- **Assessment:** no hints, no warnings. Feedback only after you submit.

The debrief records which mode and score settings were used, so attempts can be compared fairly.
