Step through moments or press **Play**. Markers show first looks at important evidence, findings, requests, hints, traps and when key findings were reached. The side panel shows your findings and evidence log as they stood.

The case author's suggested route is one good route, not the only one. Export a one page summary as Markdown or an image. The replay is built from logs already saved; nothing extra is stored.
