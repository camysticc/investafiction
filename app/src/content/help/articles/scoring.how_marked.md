- **Key findings:** each required finding found and labelled correctly; stretch findings add extra.
- **Analysis:** labelling, accepted links backed by evidence, timeline accuracy, statement conflicts found, red herrings avoided.
- **Report:** sections complete, findings cited, gaps admitted.
- **Handling:** receipt logged, no continuity gaps, hashes checked, integrity problems reported, reasons for originals.
- **Procedure:** starts at 10 and loses marks per trap.
- **Grading:** grades within one step of the expected ones.

A serious breach caps the total at 69 (Pass).
