The top bar shows the case title, mode, case clock, save status and the **Brief** button. The middle tabs hold the evidence viewer, search, statements, timeline, link chart, map, web browser, requests, integrity checker and report. After submission a **Debrief** tab appears.

You can move the evidence list to the right, or put the side panel along the bottom, in **Settings > Appearance > Panel arrangement**.
