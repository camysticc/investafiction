The case saves after every action and while you work. You can also press **Save** in the top bar or Ctrl+S.

Saves, settings, Academy progress and your profile are kept in separate small files, so a problem with one never damages the others. **Settings > Saves and data** shows where they are, how much space they use, and lets you export or import them.
