1. Open the **Case library**.
2. Choose **Import case pack** and pick the `.invcase` file.
3. Read the summary of checks, then choose **Add to library**.

Every pack is checked for safe paths, allowed file types and sizes, the hash of every file, the schemas and the cross references. If anything fails, nothing is added and the reasons are listed.
