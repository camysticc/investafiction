- **Log all** logs receipt of every item not yet received.
- **Hash all** checks every item against its acquisition hash.
- Tick items to log a selection.
- Filter by words, type and status.

Items from the newest stage are highlighted (switch off in **Settings > Gameplay and mode**). Examined items can show a tick, a faded row or both.
